# Keep track changes outside the canonical body until acceptance

Suggestions, including text, formatting, and structural block changes, are stored separately from the canonical Markdown AST and Yjs body. One `suggestion_id` is one atomic change set; accept/reject applies to the whole set, and multiple IDs can be reviewed separately. Comment access may propose; effective edit/owner access may accept or reject. Acceptance revalidates the current tree and commits one body edit; an unsafe structural rebase becomes a visible conflict. If acceptance moves or deletes an existing node, it follows the validated `MoveNode`/`DeleteNode` path: a structural change atomically increments `body_version` and `body_epoch` with the accepted status and durable receipt, and all pending work from the previous epoch stays local for review. Suggestion content and its retained history are visible only to the proposer and eligible editors who can still read the document; proposals are online-only in the first release and never feed Markdown export or RAG. AI edits to existing documents use the same approval path. This adds suggestion history and conflict handling, but prevents unapproved content from silently becoming the document or chatbot evidence.
